# Review Mining

Store and marketplace reviews are the cheapest honest voice-of-customer you will
get. The goal is counted themes with verbatim quotes, not vibes.

## Sources

| Source | Where | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| App Store | The app's listing, "Ratings & Reviews", sorted by Most Recent | Each country storefront has its own reviews. Start with your main market. |
| Google Play | The app's listing, "Ratings and reviews" | Play shows "most relevant" by default; switch to newest. |
| G2 / Capterra / Trustpilot | Product page, filter by star rating | B2B and web SaaS. Watch for incentivized reviews. |
| Reddit / communities | Search "[competitor] review", "[competitor] vs", "[competitor] alternative" | Unprompted, often harsher and more specific. |
| Competitor's own forum or feature board | Public roadmap, feature requests, upvote counts | Upvote counts are free demand data. |

## Sample

- Read the newest 50-100 reviews per competitor, or the last 6 months, whichever
  is fewer. Recent reviews describe the current product.
- Read low (1-3 star) and high (5 star) separately. Skip 4 star unless the sample
  is small.
- Note the app version in a review when shown. A complaint fixed two versions ago
  is history.

## Tag

Give each review one or more tags from a short list. Start with these and add
only when three or more reviews need a new one:

| Tag | Covers |
|-----|--------|
| `price` | Too expensive, surprise charges, paywall placement, subscription anger |
| `missing` | A feature they wanted and could not find (name the feature) |
| `bugs` | Crashes, sync loss, data loss, login failures |
| `ux` | Confusing, too many steps, cluttered, slow to do the core job |
| `support` | No reply, slow reply, unhelpful reply |
| `onboarding` | Could not get started, setup too long |
| `love-core` | Praise for the core job working well |
| `love-specific` | Praise for one specific feature (name it) |
| `switching` | Mentions coming from or leaving for another product (name it) |

## Count and quote

| Competitor | Tag | Count / sample | Share | Best verbatim quote | Link | Date |
|------------|-----|----------------|-------|---------------------|------|------|

- Share = count / sample size. Report the sample size; 4 of 10 is not 40 of 100.
- Quote verbatim, short, with a link to the review or the listing and the date.
- Never paste reviewers' names or handles into public files.

## Turn it into decisions

- A `missing` theme that shows up across two or more competitors is a parity gap
  or an open gap (see SKILL.md section 5).
- A `price` or `bugs` theme concentrated on one competitor is a "How we win" line
  on its battlecard, and a landmine question.
- `switching` mentions tell you the real competitor set. Add any product named
  three or more times.
- `love-specific` tells you what a competitor's users would miss if they switched.
  Match it or name it as a deliberate trade-off.
- Reviewers' own words are the best source for store copy and hooks. Use the job
  they describe, never the competitor's name.
